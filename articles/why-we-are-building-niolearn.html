FOUNDING ESSAY • OCTOBER 2026

Why We're Building NioLearn: Rethinking the Way Students Navigate Tech Admissions.

A reflection on the disconnect between traditional coaching and modern engineering education, and why structured guidance is the foundation of genuine opportunity.

The Divergence in Tech Admissions

For decades, the narrative surrounding engineering admissions in India has been monolithic: two to three years of high-pressure coaching, intensive memorization of inorganic chemistry reactions, and speed-trick problem solving tailored exclusively for legacy examinations.

Yet when students enter the contemporary software industry, the competencies that define success are fundamentally different. Engineering teams look for algorithmic reasoning, systematic problem decomposition, computational thinking, and the ability to articulate technical ideas clearly under ambiguity.

Over the past few years, a notable shift has begun taking shape across higher education. A new wave of project-centric, industry-aligned technology institutions has emerged—places designed from the ground up for hands-on computer science, artificial intelligence, and applied software development. But as new pathways emerged, so did profound confusion.

The Problem: Fragmentation and Broken Guidance

When students and their parents begin researching alternative and next-generation computer science pathways, they quickly encounter an ecosystem characterized by noise rather than clarity:

  • Information Asymmetry: Each institution conducts its own distinct diagnostic evaluation, aptitude test, and interview process. Families have had no central, verified benchmark to understand which pathways match a student's strengths.
  • The Preparation Void: Traditional coaching institutes remain anchored strictly to conventional rote curricula, offering zero support for modern computational aptitude, data interpretation, or algorithmic interviews.
  • Distorted Commercial Incentives: Much of the admissions advice currently available online is steered by commissioned agents and marketing affiliates who prioritize referral fees over genuine pedagogical fit.
  • The Exhaustion of Starting from Scratch: Aspiring students are forced to independently reinvent their preparation strategies for every separate test and interview panel they encounter.

Having mentored over 600 students in mathematics and systems thinking throughout my own academic journey, I saw firsthand how much anxiety this structural confusion caused. Students with exceptional analytical potential were feeling sidelined, not because they lacked capability, but because the guidance infrastructure had failed to evolve alongside modern opportunities.

The Founding Thesis Behind NioLearn

We founded NioLearn with a straightforward conviction: navigating modern technology admissions should be structured, honest, and rigorous.

Rather than treating every emerging admission pathway as an isolated hurdle, NioLearn approaches tech admissions as an integrated preparation ecosystem. The core competencies that top technology institutions assess—Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Algorithmic Thinking, and Communicative Clarity—share deep structural commonalities.

"When you prepare for underlying principles rather than arbitrary exam patterns, you don't just crack an admission process—you develop the mental models required to succeed as an engineer."

To build something truly substantive, we assembled a founding team that brings together complementary strengths:

  • Academics & Pedagogy: Led by Lokesh Sir (Co-Founder & Head of Academics) in collaboration with Modulus Classes, bringing over 16 years of teaching experience and academic rigor to curriculum design.
  • Product & Platform Systems: Led by Abhiraj Jain (Co-Founder & Head of Product & Technology), engineering calibrated diagnostic tooling and responsive learning interfaces.
  • Operations & Cohort Delivery: Led by Aayush Bhatt (Co-Founder & Head of Operations & Delivery), ensuring meticulous batch coordination, scheduling discipline, and personalized student support.
  • Business & Strategic Expansion: Led by myself, Sidhant Pande (Co-Founder & Head of Business), directing ecosystem partnerships, institutional research, cohort growth, and long-term strategy.

What NioLearn Delivers

NioLearn is designed as a focused, live preparation ecosystem rather than an impersonal video library:

  • Structured Live Cohorts: Small, capped batches (under 40 students) where instructors know every learner by name and track individual conceptual progress.
  • Calibrated Diagnostic Assessments: Benchmarking quantitative aptitude and logical deduction against real entrance parameters.
  • Algorithmic Logic Over Rote Memorization: Teaching foundational computational thinking that applies directly to modern computer science admissions.
  • 1:1 Interview Clinics: Guiding students through technical discussions, portfolio reviews, and admission interviews without artificial scripts.
  • Institutional Intelligence: Transparent, verified data on curriculums, faculty, campus infrastructure, and genuine outcome profiles.

Our founding cohort is scheduled to commence on October 24, 2026, beginning with students targeting 2027 admissions across forward-thinking institutions.

Navigate More Possibilities

The tagline of NioLearn is "Navigate More Possibilities." It is more than a marketing slogan; it is an operating philosophy.

Technology education in India is no longer a single, narrow corridor where a fraction of a percent find success and the rest are left behind. There are exciting, rigorous, and deeply rewarding alternatives emerging every year. What students need is not more pressure—they need a compass, a rigorous training ground, and mentors who genuinely care about where they land.

That is why we are building NioLearn.
